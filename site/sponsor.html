Frequently Asked Questions

How long does onboarding take? ▼
Most sponsors are live within 24-48 hours. We handle all technical setup, creative review, and integration with our platform. Our support team will guide you through every step.
Can I track performance in real-time? ▼
Yes! All plans include a real-time analytics dashboard. Track impressions, clicks, installations, user demographics, and more. Professional and Enterprise plans get advanced insights and custom reports.
What if I need to modify my campaign? ▼
Campaigns are flexible! You can pause, resume, or modify your ads anytime from your dashboard. For creative changes or custom requirements, contact your account manager.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major credit cards, ACH transfers, and wire transfers. Enterprise clients have additional payment options available. Invoices are issued monthly.
Is there a minimum commitment period? ▼
No! All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitments. You can cancel anytime. That said, most sponsors see better results with sustained campaigns over 3+ months.
Who do I contact for Enterprise plans? ▼
Reach out to our sales team at sponsor@rightskills.com with details about your needs. We'll connect you with an account executive to discuss custom packages, volume pricing, and partnership opportunities.
